ABOUT ME

Design meets implementation.

Prototyping is how I work: I reduce an idea to its important rules, implement it, playtest it and iterate. Because most of my portfolio projects were built independently and within a limited timeframe, they deliberately focus on selected mechanics instead of imitating a full production.

In a studio, I want to bring that end-to-end perspective into a multidisciplinary team, deepen my Unity/C# work and contribute wherever game design and programming meet.

I am currently based near Frankfurt, but I search beyond the region and am open to relocating for the right opportunity. On-site, hybrid and remote positions are all options for me.

CASE STUDY · UNITY / C#

Don’t Look

A first-person psychological horror prototype where everyday apartment tasks become the structure for tension, interruptions and environmental storytelling.

DESIGN CHALLENGE

Guide without breaking immersion

The player needs to understand a fixed sequence of tasks while mouse-look and exploration remain available. Contextual thoughts communicate the next goal and explain locked interactions.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Interaction flow and implementation

  • Designed the 19-step task sequence
  • Built pickup, carrying and placement rules
  • Added contextual thoughts and early-action feedback
  • Implemented timed microwave, watering and mirror events
PROJECT STRUCTURE

Two core responsibilities

Player movement
input · look · locomotion → Interactions
tasks · objects · thoughts
GDD SNAPSHOT

Documented rules

Core loop 19-step task flow Held-item rules Feedback language Horror beats Audio / VFX triggers

SpaceBastion

A team project where I was responsible for the weather and lighting systems that shape the game world’s atmosphere.

DESIGN CHALLENGE

MY CONTRIBUTION

CASE STUDY · OPENFRAMEWORKS / C++

Vertical Shooter

A self-contained learning project that connects combat, enemy variety, upgrades, boss scaling and persistence into one replayable loop.

DESIGN CHALLENGE

Keep escalation readable

Three enemy archetypes with one, two and three hit points establish a clear difficulty language. Boss encounters and upgrades add longer-term decisions.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Full system design and build

  • Combat and three enemy types
  • Boss health: 10 + 5 × level
  • Shop for fire rate, multishot and maximum HP
  • Level progression and persistent highscore file
